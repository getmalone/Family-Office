Import accounts & positions

Bulk-load opening positions from a CSV, JSON, or XML export (format auto-detected). Required fields: account and quantity. Optional: account_type, institution, symbol, name, asset_class, cost_basis_total, cost_per_share, acquired, price.

Re-uploading replaces the previously imported holdings in the accounts the file names, so an updated export won't stack a second copy on top. Accounts not in the file, hand-entered transactions, and lots you've already sold from are left untouched.

Example CSV 
account,account_type,symbol,name,asset_class,quantity,cost_basis_total,acquired,price
My Brokerage,brokerage,VOO,Vanguard S&P 500 ETF,us_equity,100,45000,2023-03-15,520.50
My Roth IRA,ira_roth,AAPL,Apple Inc.,us_equity,50,8000,2022-06-01,195.20

      ] }
  ]
}
