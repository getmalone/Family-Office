Spouse's Social Security — leave blank if single / no spousal benefit

Enter each person's estimated monthly benefit at their full retirement age (from the SSA statement), adjusted for the claiming age you pick. The lower earner is automatically topped up toward 50% of the higher earner's benefit (spousal), the survivor keeps the larger of the two, and the income bridge nets both streams against the pre-SSA gap. Set FRA to 66 for anyone born before 1960.

{# ── Income Bridge: account-type withdrawal sequencing ── #}
Income Bridge — which accounts fund the pre-SSA years

Splits the portfolio into taxable, tax-deferred and Roth buckets and spends them in that order, netting Social Security first. Tax-deferred withdrawals are grossed up by the rate above (so they cost more to spend); Roth is tax-free. Leave the $ overrides blank to use your account balances.

Max sustainable spending

The highest spending that keeps the portfolio's survival rate at your target — using the scenario above (ages, Social Security, contributions, distribution years, spending pattern).
